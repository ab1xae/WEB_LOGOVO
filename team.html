Who Cuts Your Hair

In LOGOVO there are three levels of barbers: lead, top and senior. The level depends on the experience of the barber and the price depends on the level.

Every barber works 3 years or more. Below are some of our barbers of each level and the prices. You can choose a barber or just the level in the booking form.

Prices are in tenge (tg). The full price list with all services is on the Services & Prices page.
