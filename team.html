Who Cuts Your Hair

In LOGOVO there are two levels of barbers: top and senior. The level depends on the experience of the barber and the price depends on the level.

Every barber works 3 years or more. Below are the barbers of each level and the prices. You can choose the level of the barber in the booking form.

Prices are in tenge (tg). The full price list with all services is on the Services & Prices page.
