Our Services

In LOGOVO (the word logovo means "lair" in Russian) the price depends on the barber's level: top or senior. A haircut from a top or senior barber includes a free bonus. The steps of a visit are in "How a Visit Works".

Every Tuesday is Student Day: a men's haircut costs 500 tg less. On the price list it is called Student Day. Prices are in tenge (tg), for example 3000–5000 tg for a men's haircut. Kids pay less.

  • Haircuts

    • Kids (up to 7 years)
    • Men's (8 years and older)
    • Long hair

  • Beard

    • Beard styling
    • Beard tinting

  • Other

    • Hair tinting
    • Facial cleansing
    • Perm + haircut

How a Visit Works

  1. Choose a service and a barber in the booking form.
  2. Come at your time to Mangilik El 51/2.
  3. The barber asks what you want and does the job.
  4. With a top or senior barber you get the free bonus.
