Who We Are

LOGOVO is a men's barbershop in Astana with 20 experienced barbers, each with 3 years of experience or more. We have more than 2000 reviews on 2GIS with a rating of 4.9, and in 2025 we got two 2GIS Awards. Our main rule is attention to every client. Our senior barber talks about it in the interview below.
