Two Years of LOGOVO

LOGOVO opened in 2024. At the start it was a small men's barbershop with a few chairs. The idea was simple: a place where a man comes not only for a haircut, but also to sit down, relax and leave looking good.

In two years the small shop grew into a team of 20 barbers, more than 2000 reviews on 2GIS with a rating of 4.9. In 2026 LOGOVO was named the best barbershop of Astana.

Why LOGOVO

The word logovo means lair, the den of a wild animal. The name was chosen because it sounds brutal, and brutal is exactly the style the owners wanted for a men's barbershop. The emblem of the shop is a wolf, and a wolf lives in a lair, so the name and the emblem go together. If you look at the sign, the letters L and O are drawn as straight razors, the main tool of a barber.

The wolf
Strength and loyalty to the pack. The wolf is on the wall, on the cups and on the sign.
The lair
A place where a man feels at home. That is why there is a waiting area with a sofa, a TV and a game console.
The razor letters
The barber's straight razor became the letters of the name.
