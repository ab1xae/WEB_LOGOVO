Welcome to LOGOVO

LOGOVO Barbershop is in Astana, at Mangilik El 51/1. We have 20 barbers, and each of them has worked for 3 years or more. We have more than 2000 reviews on 2GIS with a rating of 5.0. In 2025 we got two 2GIS Awards as the best barbershop in our category. We do haircuts for kids and adults, beard styling, hair and beard tinting, facial cleansing and perms. We are open every day from 10:00 to 21:00.

On this site you can see our services and prices, book a visit, read about us and an interview with our senior barber, and look at photos from inside the barbershop.
